Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Summary: HopSites is built with privacy as a foundational principle. We do not collect, track, store, or transmit your personal data, browsing history, or device information. No account is required, and we operate zero analytics, telemetry, or advertising frameworks.

1. Overview

HopSites is a focused Spaces-based web browser and site organiser designed and developed by Hassan Javed (FromHaz) for iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later.

This privacy policy explains how HopSites handles your information. In short: your web browsing, saved Spaces, and library belong entirely to you and never pass through our servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

HopSites does not collect or process any personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts or registration: You never need to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use HopSites.
  • No browsing history collection: We do not track, log, or monitor the websites you visit, the searches you perform, or the links you tap.
  • No analytics or telemetry: We do not include any third-party analytics SDKs, diagnostics services, crash reporting trackers, or usage monitors.
  • No advertising or tracking: HopSites does not display third-party advertisements and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not access Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No proxying or inspection: All web requests made in HopSites connect directly from your device to the destination server using Apple’s standard WebKit engine. We operate no intermediary proxies or servers.

3. Web Browsing & Content Blocking

When you browse the web using HopSites, pages are rendered using Apple’s native WebKit engine (the same secure platform engine that powers Safari):

  • Direct Connection: Your device communicates directly with the websites you choose to visit. Those websites receive your standard web request (such as your IP address and user-agent) as they would in any web browser.
  • Native Content Blocker: HopSites includes a local, offline rule-based content blocker powered by Apple’s WKContentRuleList. This stops known ad networks, trackers, and intrusive scripts from loading. The rule list operates completely offline on your device, and no browsing activity is reported back to us.
  • Cookies & Website Data: Website cookies and caches are managed locally by iOS WebKit and are isolated within your app container.

4. Local Storage & Private iCloud Sync

Your library—including custom Spaces, site titles, URLs, and display arrangements—is saved locally on your device:

  • Local Core Data Storage: All data is stored in your device’s sandboxed app directory.
  • Private Apple iCloud Sync: When iCloud is enabled on your device, HopSites syncs your Spaces and sites across your Apple devices using Apple’s CloudKit in your private iCloud database (iCloud.com.fromhaz.sites). This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple, tied to your personal Apple Account, and is strictly inaccessible to FromHaz or any third party.

5. Apple App Privacy Nutrition Details

In accordance with Apple’s App Store Privacy Requirements:

  • Data Used to Track You: None.
  • Data Linked to You: None.
  • Data Not Linked to You: None.
  • Data Collection: Data Not Collected.

6. Children’s Privacy

Because HopSites does not collect, request, or store any personal data from any user, it does not collect information from children under any jurisdiction.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy in the future, the revised document will be published immediately on this page with an updated effective date.
