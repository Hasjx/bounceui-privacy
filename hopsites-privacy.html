Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

HopSites does not collect, track, or share any personal data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data, device information, or usage analytics. There are no accounts, no tracking tools, and no advertisements.

Web Browsing

When you browse the web in HopSites, your device connects directly to the websites you visit. We do not operate servers, proxies, or logging mechanisms to monitor or record your browsing activity.

Storage & Sync

Your Spaces and saved websites are stored locally on your device. If you use iCloud, your data syncs securely through your personal Apple Account using Apple’s CloudKit. We cannot access your iCloud data.
