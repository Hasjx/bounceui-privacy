Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026

Summary: BounceUI is a completely offline application. We do not collect, track, store, or transmit any personal data, usage metrics, or device information. No account is required, and the app makes zero network connections.

1. Overview

BounceUI is an offline SwiftUI interaction and motion laboratory designed and published by Hassan Javed (FromHaz) for iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later.

This privacy policy explains how BounceUI treats your privacy when you install and use the application. The short answer is simple: your data belongs entirely to you and never leaves your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

BounceUI has been built from the ground up to operate without data collection of any kind. Specifically:

  • No accounts or registration: You are never asked to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address, name, or password.
  • No network requests: The application does not connect to any servers, APIs, or internet endpoints. It functions completely offline.
  • No analytics or diagnostics: We do not bundle third-party analytics frameworks, crash reporting tools, or usage trackers. We cannot see which modules you explore, how long you use them, or what parameters you test.
  • No tracking: BounceUI does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not access Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No advertising: There are no third-party advertisements, promotional banners, or ad networks embedded in the app.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions: There are no payment processors, paywalls, or financial transactions inside the app.

3. Local Device Storage

BounceUI allows you to customise module parameters and save preferred presets. Any configuration, preset, or code snippet you generate or save is stored strictly locally on your device using local storage provided by iOS.

This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the application from your device, all locally stored presets are permanently removed along with the app container, as managed by iOS.

4. System Requirements

BounceUI requires iOS 26 or later to take full advantage of native SwiftUI Liquid Glass, fluid physics, and modern system rendering capabilities.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because BounceUI does not collect, request, or store any personal data from any user, it does not collect information from children under any jurisdiction.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever update this policy in the future, the revised document will be published immediately on this page with an updated effective date.
