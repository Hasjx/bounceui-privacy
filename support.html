About BounceUI

Apple’s documentation tells you what an interface does. BounceUI lets you touch it: tap it, drag it, turn its numbers up and down, and copy the exact SwiftUI code that makes it work.

Designed for designers and iOS engineers, BounceUI brings 48 parameterised specimens spanning Foundations, Motion, Components, Inputs, Glass, and Patterns directly under your finger on iOS 26 and later.

How Can We Help?

If you have encountered an issue, have feedback on a specific module specimen, or wish to suggest a new SwiftUI component or pattern for inclusion, we are always glad to hear from you.

Submitting a Bug Report

When reporting an unexpected visual artefact or interaction bug, please consider including:

  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 17 Pro, iPad Pro 13-inch).
  • Your iOS version (e.g. iOS 26.0).
  • The name of the module or specimen where the issue occurred.
  • A brief description of what you expected to see versus what happened.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BounceUI require an internet connection?

No. BounceUI is 100% offline. All specimens, interactive stages, physics calculations, and code generators run entirely locally on your device without communicating over the network.

Does BounceUI collect any diagnostic or usage data?

None at all. BounceUI contains zero analytics, tracking, advertising, or background telemetry. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details.

Where are my saved presets stored?

All saved presets and parameters are stored exclusively in your device’s local sandboxed app container. They are never synced to any remote server.

What are the minimum system requirements?

BounceUI requires iOS 26.0 or later on compatible iPhone and iPad models.
