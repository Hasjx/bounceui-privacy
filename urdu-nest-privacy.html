Urdu Nest privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Native iOS version

No data sent

Urdu Nest is provided by Hassan Javed under the FromHaz name. The iOS app works offline. It does not send learning data to us or other organisations. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, microphone access, speech recording or purchases.

What stays on the device

The app stores saved words, reading preference, exposures, correct and incorrect responses, mastery and practice dates, completed lessons and units, unfinished activities, learning days, daily goals, family activities, feathers and Nest decorations. The optional script track keeps separate written-form progress and the Roman clue preference. This information resumes learning, selects local review and shows progress. Family activities are self-confirmed; no speech is recorded or verified.

Clear progress

A grown-up can open Family settings → Clear local progress to delete local learning history, saved words, unfinished activities and rewards. The main reading preference stays the same. We have no remote copy to delete. Removing the app normally removes local data; restoring a device backup may bring saved preferences back.

Device backup and the App Store

Apple’s backup and migration features may include local app preferences, according to your family’s device settings. Apple and the App Store handle their own download, security and diagnostic information under their policies. Urdu Nest has no app-operated server or remote tracking.

Children and families

The app does not ask for a child’s name, birthday, email address or identifier. Parent Corner uses a simple arithmetic gate to separate grown-up settings; it is not a password or authentication system. The app has no links leaving the learning experience, public profiles, social sharing, competitive rankings or purchases.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, a grown-up can email hassan@fromhaz.com. Email is outside the app and voluntary; sending an email shares your email address and the message you choose to send. Please do not include a child’s personal details. Support messages are used to respond to your request.

This website

These pages do not include analytics, tracking scripts, advertising or embedded third-party media. The website hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and secure the site. Visiting the site is separate from using the offline app.
