Urdu Nest support

Little Urdu lessons to try with your family. Roman Urdu comes first, with English explanations and Urdu script alongside it.

Contact a grown-up can use

Email hassan@fromhaz.com for help. Include your device model, iOS version and what happened. Please do not send a child’s personal details. There is no account or learning-data upload to look up.

Where do I start?

Open Learn and choose the current lesson. The path chooses the next activity. Practice brings back words that need another try. Words is a reference library; Progress shows mastery and your Nest.

Do I need to read Urdu script?

No. The main course can be completed using Roman Urdu. Learn Urdu script is a separate optional track. A grown-up can change reading style in Family settings; Roman Urdu is never hidden automatically.

Why is there no sound?

This version is silent. It has no pronunciation recordings, speech checking or microphone access. Family challenges invite you to try the words with someone at home, then mark them as tried.

Where is progress saved?

Progress stays on the device. There is no account or app-operated cloud sync. Apple’s device backup or migration may include local preferences according to your family’s settings.

How do I open grown-up settings?

Choose For grown-ups and answer the short arithmetic question. Family settings contains the privacy policy, reading choices, progress summary and local reset. The gate separates settings from learning; it is not a password.

How can I start again?

A grown-up can use Family settings → Clear local progress. This removes learning history, saved words, unfinished activities and rewards. The main reading preference stays the same.

Privacy

Urdu Nest works offline with no accounts, ads, analytics or purchases. Read the privacy policy.
