Dayfetti Support

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Look forward to a date. Keep the memories that follow.

Create and personalise a countdown

Tap New on Upcoming, enter a name and date, then Add. Choose an all-day date or an exact start time, with optional recurrence and reminders. Appearance's Done button returns to your draft; Add or Save on the main event form commits it. Duplicate opens an unsaved copy so you can change it before adding another event.

Settings → Card appearance changes app backgrounds automatically for cards using App default. Individual overrides stay intact. Category presets initialise new cards; updating existing cards requires a separate confirmed action.

Keep a memory

Past one-off events appear in Moments. Add private words and photos, or use Moments' plus button to keep a past day. Small swipeable previews open larger galleries. A repeating event can keep a separate past occasion without changing its series or copying its reminders.

Archive, delete and recover

Swipe an Upcoming card to archive or recoverably delete it. Settings → Recovery contains Archived and Recently Deleted. Deleted content has no automatic expiry and stays recoverable until you confirm permanent deletion. Permanently deleted content cannot be restored through Dayfetti.

Do not uninstall or reset the app as a troubleshooting step if you need its local data.

Home and Lock Screen widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, use the system's Add Widget action and find Dayfetti. Edit a placed widget to select an event. The in-app guide is under Settings → Home & Lock Screen → Widgets. iOS manages placement and refresh; widgets do not run a second-by-second clock.

Reminders and Calendar import

Set reminders from an event. Settings → Reminders shows notification permission status and a link to iOS Settings when needed. Focus and notification settings can affect when an alert is shown.

Calendar import copies selected dates once and never edits your original calendar. Repeating sources offer their next date as an explicit one-off. Imported copies are recognised by source identity, even after you rename the copy in Dayfetti.

Free and Dayfetti Pro

Countdowns, memories, photos, widgets, colours and patterns, reminders, sharing, Calendar import and recovery are available free. Pro is an optional lifetime upgrade for exact card/countdown colours and an expanded collection of patterns, symbols and emoji. Existing premium appearances remain accessible if ownership changes.

Settings → Dayfetti Pro offers Restore Purchases and Redeem Code beneath buying. Restore uses the Apple Account that owns the purchase. A code unlocks Pro only after Apple verifies ownership. Apple manages payments, eligible refunds and code availability; contact Apple Support for billing help.

Where your data lives

This release stores your events and photos on the device. iCloud sync is not enabled. There is no FromHaz account or independent event-backup/export service. Device backups follow your Apple settings; do not rely on unverified backup or sync. Read the privacy policy for details.

Contact support

Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, system version and the steps that led to the problem. Share personal event details or photos only if you choose to; they are not sent automatically. Please leave out passwords and unnecessary sensitive information.
