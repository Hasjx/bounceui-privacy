Dayfetti Support

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Look forward to a date. Keep the memories that follow.

Create and personalise a countdown

Tap + New on Upcoming, enter a name and date, then tap Add. Choose an all-day date or an exact start time, with optional recurrence (including custom intervals) and reminders. Duplicate opens an unsaved copy so you can adjust it before saving.

Settings → Appearance controls the app theme, Default Card Style and the live timer. Category designs set the starting look for new cards; updating existing cards in a category always asks for confirmation first.

Keep a memory and Moments Lock

Past one-off events move into Moments automatically, or you can move today's one-off card sooner from its menu. Save a memory with optional photos and words. A repeating event can save a memory for a past occasion without changing its future schedule or reminders.

With Dayfetti Pro, you can turn on Lock Moments in Settings to protect Moments and private memory content on that device using Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.

Archive, delete and 30-day recovery

Swipe or use a card's menu to archive or delete it. Archived and Recently Deleted cards live under Settings → Archived & Deleted (and the archive button in Moments). Deleted cards stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days before being removed permanently, or until you confirm permanent deletion sooner. Permanently deleted cards cannot be recovered through Dayfetti.

Home and Lock Screen widgets

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and find Dayfetti. Edit a placed widget to follow your next date, a category, your favourites or a single event. See Settings → Widgets for the in-app guide. iOS manages widget placement and refresh.

Reminders and Calendar import

New countdowns and Calendar imports use Settings → Default reminder (which you can change or set to None). Settings → Reminders pauses or resumes all notifications and shows permission status when needed.

Calendar import copies selected dates once and never modifies your source calendar. Yearly dates such as birthdays can import as yearly countdowns; other repeating sources offer their next date as a one-off.

Free and Dayfetti Pro

Unlimited countdowns, iCloud sync, widgets, reminders, photos, Calendar import, backups, 30-day recovery and sharing are free. Dayfetti Pro is a single one-time purchase (never a subscription) for Moments Lock, to do lists, unlimited custom categories, any colour, the full pattern and symbol collections, and sharing without the Dayfetti mark. Existing saved styles and items always stay usable if Pro ownership changes.

Settings → Dayfetti Pro offers Restore Purchases and Redeem Code beneath buying. Apple manages payments, eligible refunds and code availability; contact Apple Support for billing help.

iCloud sync and backups

When iCloud is enabled for Dayfetti in your device settings, your library syncs privately across your devices under your Apple Account. Please keep Dayfetti updated on all your devices so older versions do not drop newer card settings when editing a card.

Settings → iCloud & Backups keeps automatic local safety copies on your device and lets you export or restore .dayfettibackup files anytime. Restoring a backup adds missing cards without overwriting cards that already exist. Before deleting the app, export a backup first. Read the privacy policy for details, including how to turn off anonymous usage analytics in Settings → Help & About.

Contact support

Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, system version and the steps that led to the problem. Share personal event details or photos only if you choose to; they are not sent automatically. Please leave out passwords and unnecessary sensitive information.
