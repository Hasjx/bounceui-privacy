About HopSites

HopSites is a modern web browser built around the concept of Spaces. Rather than managing sprawling tabs, HopSites allows you to organize your essential websites into dedicated Spaces, swipe seamlessly between live pages, and enjoy a clutter-free browsing experience on iOS 26 and later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does HopSites track or collect my browsing history?

No. HopSites operates zero analytics, tracking, or telemetry. Your browsing history is completely private to your device and is never sent to our servers. Review our Privacy Policy for full details.

How does iCloud sync work?

HopSites uses Apple’s native CloudKit technology. Your saved Spaces, site titles, and custom layouts are synced directly through your personal Apple Account. Data is encrypted end-to-end by Apple and is not accessible by FromHaz.

How do I swipe between sites?

When you are viewing any site within a Space, simply swipe horizontally from the left or right edge of the screen to transition fluidly to the next or previous site in that Space.

What are the minimum system requirements?

HopSites requires iOS 26.0 or iPadOS 26.0 or later on compatible iPhone and iPad devices.
