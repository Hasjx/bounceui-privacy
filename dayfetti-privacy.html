Dayfetti Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Dayfetti is a countdown and private keepsake app by Hassan Javed / FromHaz. No Dayfetti account, advertising or cross-app tracking is required. Your events, notes, to do lists, memories and photos stay on your devices and sync privately through your own iCloud account.

Your dates, photos and preferences

Dayfetti stores event titles, dates, time zones, categories, customisations, reminder preferences, notes, to do lists and memories on your device and, when iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud database. FromHaz does not receive this content or operate a server-side event database.

The system photo picker shares only the images you select. Dayfetti does not require broad photo-library access to select a photo. Imported copies are resized and re-encoded without their original location or camera metadata; your originals in Photos are unchanged. Private memory photos are separate from countdown artwork and have smaller thumbnails for browsing.

Storage, iCloud sync, recovery and backups

When iCloud is turned on for Dayfetti in your device settings, your library syncs privately across your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) under your Apple Account. Archived events remain in Archived & Deleted until you restore or delete them. Deleted events stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days before being permanently removed, or until you confirm permanent deletion sooner.

Dayfetti also keeps automatic local safety copies on your device and lets you export and restore backup files (.dayfettibackup) whenever you choose. Removing the app removes its local data from that device.

Moments Lock (Face ID, Touch ID and passcode)

With Dayfetti Pro, you can lock Moments on a device using Apple's Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS; Dayfetti never receives biometric data or your passcode, and the lock setting is stored locally on each device.

Anonymous usage analytics

Dayfetti collects anonymous usage statistics (such as app opens, feature usage counts, device type and error events) through PostHog (EU Cloud) to help improve reliability and features. This data uses a random anonymous install identifier with no cross-app tracking, no advertising identifiers (IDFA) and no session replay. Your event titles, dates, notes, to do lists, memories, category names and photos are never sent. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Help & About → Share Anonymous Analytics.

Calendar access

Calendar access is optional and is used to let you select upcoming dates for one-time import. Dayfetti does not modify your source calendar or provide continuous two-way synchronisation. Imported occurrence identifiers are saved locally to recognise repeat imports. Repeating calendar sources offer a selected next date as an explicit one-off.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders use optional local notifications. Event titles may appear in notification previews according to your iOS notification privacy settings.

Widgets read a local App Group snapshot shared with the app. Countdown titles, dates and appearance can be visible on a Home or Lock Screen where you place a widget. Widgets currently omit photo bytes and use the system's sensitive-content presentation.

Sharing you choose

A shared countdown image can contain its title, category, date, countdown, appearance, enabled milestone label, chosen message and optional watermark. A selected artwork photo can appear in that image. Event notes, the original milestone year, private memory words and private memory photos are excluded from countdown cards.

The memory-photo viewer also offers a native share or save action for the individual photo you choose. The destination you select handles the shared image according to its own practices. Dayfetti does not upload your images to a FromHaz server.

Optional Pro purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases, restoration, eligible refunds and offer-code redemption. Dayfetti reads verified StoreKit entitlements, including Apple's signed local ownership records, to determine access. FromHaz does not receive your payment-card details. See Apple's App Store privacy information.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and investigate your request. Support correspondence is retained while needed for that request and related follow-up, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations. You can ask about deletion by contacting us. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

These policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web-request information to GitHub. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to these pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.
